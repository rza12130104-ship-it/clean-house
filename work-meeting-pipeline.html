Case Study

Meeting-to-Action Pipeline for a Professional Services Firm

Client calls were generating great notes and no follow-through. We built a pipeline that turns every calendar meeting into a documented summary, a tracked action list, and a drafted follow-up email — automatically.

Client Type
Professional services firm
Team Size
6 client-facing consultants
Build Time
~2 weeks
Category
Workflow Automation
The Problem

Good calls, forgotten follow-ups

Consultants took notes in whatever app was open — a notebook, a doc, sometimes nothing. Action items lived in someone's memory. Follow-up emails went out late, or not at all, and there was no shared record of what was promised to which client.

~40%
Meetings with no written follow-up
3-5 days
Typical delay before recap sent
0
Central action-item tracker
The Workflow

What happens when a meeting ends

1

Calendar event closes

The end of a scheduled client meeting is the trigger — no one has to remember to kick anything off.

2

Meeting doc generated from a template

A structured summary doc is created in Drive, pre-filled with the client name, attendees, and date, ready for notes.

3

Action items extracted to a tracker

Bulleted action items from the notes are pulled into a shared Airtable tracker with an owner and due date, visible to the whole team.

4

Follow-up email drafted

A recap email addressed to the client is drafted in Gmail, summarizing what was discussed and what happens next — ready for a quick review and send.

5

Next touchpoint scheduled

A reminder is set ahead of the next milestone or check-in date so no client goes quiet for too long.
