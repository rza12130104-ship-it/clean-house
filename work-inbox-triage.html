Case Study

Inbox Triage & Auto-Draft for a Small Support Team

A two-person support team was drowning in 100+ emails a day — sales questions, support tickets, and spam all mixed together. We built a triage layer that sorts, routes, and drafts replies before a human ever opens the inbox.

Client Type
SaaS support team

The Problem

Every email got the same treatment

Sales leads, refund requests, bug reports, and newsletter replies all landed in one inbox with no sorting. The team spent the first two hours of every day just reading and routing messages before doing any actual support work — and urgent issues sometimes sat behind low-priority ones.

The Workflow

What happens when an email lands

1

Incoming mail is classified

Rules and keyword/intent matching sort each message into a category — support, sales, billing, or noise — the moment it arrives.

2

Auto-labeled and routed

Each email is labeled in Gmail and, for support issues, mirrored into a Zoho Desk ticket assigned to the right queue.

3

Reply drafted automatically

For common questions — pricing, refund policy, account access — a reply draft is generated and saved for review, never sent blind.

4

Human reviews and sends

The team opens their queue to find pre-sorted, pre-drafted emails — they edit and hit send instead of starting from a blank page.

5

Urgent messages escalate instantly

Anything flagged as angry, urgent, or high-value bypasses the queue and pings the team directly.
